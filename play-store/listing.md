## Full description (4000 characters max)

BoardFlow is a play journal for board gamers. It logs your games, remembers who won, and keeps your BoardGameGeek collection close at hand.

LOG A PLAY IN SECONDS
- Pick a game, tap who played, enter the scores and mark the winner
- Scan a score sheet with your camera and let AI fill in the players and scores (uses your own Gemini API key)
- Plays are saved on your phone first and posted to BoardGameGeek when you're online
- Add moods and a quote to remember how the session felt

YOUR JOURNAL
- Every play with players, scores and winners at a glance
- Stats for your plays and your collection: play history, top games, win rates and head-to-head records
- Challenges like "play 10 games this month", with reminders before the deadline
- Player profiles with their own colours

YOUR COLLECTION
- Your BoardGameGeek collection with ratings, player counts and play time
- Filters for owned, wishlist, played and unplayed games, and for the best player count
- Sleeve sizes for every game, with how many sleeves you need and which ones you already own
- Update your BGG collection status and rating from the game page

QUICK SETUP GUIDES
- Step-by-step setup for your games, adjusted for player count and expansions
- An "easy to forget" list, and a play timer that starts with the game

MORE
- Share a play with friends as a QR code
- Optional Google Sheets sync and Drive folders for your collection
- Home screen widgets for your last session, stats and daily insights
- Backup and restore when you move to a new phone

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
BoardFlow has no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. Your data stays on your phone and in the accounts you connect yourself.

BoardFlow is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by BoardGameGeek.
